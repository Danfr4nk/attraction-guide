Map what you find attractive. Numerically.

Iterative A/B testing of synthetic faces. Broad archetypes first, then single-variable pairs. Every face is measured in-browser with facial landmarks — ratios, not adjectives.

Rules

  • Eye color is pinned. Every face in the bank is brown-eyed. It is never the deciding variable.
  • Phase 1 — pick/rank 1 of 4 structural archetypes: wide-angular, long-narrow, heart, round.
  • Phase 2 — infinite pairs, one micro-variable each: jaw, lips, eye spacing, brows, nose.
  • Rank mode: click faces in order of preference, then lock the ranking. Richer than single-pick.
  • Confidence: 1 pick = weak · 2 consistent = leaning · 3+ = confirmed.
